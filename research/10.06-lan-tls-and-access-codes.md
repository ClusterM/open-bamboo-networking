### 10.6. LAN TLS and Studio certificate files

Studio ships two PEM files under `<resources>/cert/` for `set_cert_file`:

| File | Role |
|------|------|
| **`slicer_base64.cer`** | Named in the ABI call; BBL / RapidSSL-style bundle for **cloud** hostnames |
| **`printer.cer`** | BBL **CA** bundle (root + intermediates). **Not** the printer device leaf. Observed Studio v02.07 bundles omit per-model issuers such as `BBL Device CA N7-V2` |

**LAN device leaf (observed P2S / N7):** TLS presents a single cert `CN=<serial>`, no IP/DNS SAN; SNI = serial; per-model Device CA often missing from `printer.cer`. Intended client checks (CA + `CN=<serial>`, leaf pin when chain incomplete) and the **access code** story: **[§6 Printer channels](06-channels.md)**. SSDP: [§6.1](06.01-ssdp.md).

Stock closed-source LAN TLS verify policy is not reverse-engineered from the binary.

FTPS dialect: [§6.3](06.03-ftps.md). `:6000` / RTSPS use the same leaf family: [§6.4](06.04-port-6000.md), [§6.5](06.05-rtsp.md).

Hub: [§10](10-non-developer-mode-signing.md).
